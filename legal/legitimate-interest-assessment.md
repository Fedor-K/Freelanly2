# Legitimate Interest Assessment (LIA)
## Processing of Publicly Posted Job Opportunities from Social Media

**Document Version:** 1.0
**Last Updated:** January 2025
**Data Controller:** Freelanly

---

## 1. Purpose of Processing

### 1.1 What processing is being assessed?
Collection, storage, and display of job opportunities and freelance project requests that individuals and companies have publicly posted on social media platforms (primarily LinkedIn).

### 1.2 Data elements processed:
- Name of the person posting the opportunity
- Professional headline/title
- Profile photo (if publicly available)
- LinkedIn profile URL
- Content of the job posting
- Contact information included in the post (email, URLs)

### 1.3 Purpose of processing:
- To aggregate publicly posted job opportunities in one searchable platform
- To help job seekers discover relevant opportunities
- To increase visibility of job postings for the benefit of the original posters
- To provide job alert notifications to registered users

---

## 2. Three-Part Legitimate Interest Test

### Part A: Purpose Test

**Is the purpose legitimate?**

YES. The purposes are legitimate because:

1. **Benefit to data subjects (job posters):**
   - Individuals post job opportunities publicly with the explicit intent to reach potential candidates
   - Aggregation increases their reach beyond their immediate network
   - This serves their original purpose of finding suitable candidates

2. **Benefit to job seekers:**
   - Centralized discovery of opportunities across multiple sources
   - Time savings vs. manually searching multiple platforms
   - Relevant matching through alerts and filters

3. **No deceptive or harmful purpose:**
   - We do not misrepresent the source of data
   - We link back to original posts
   - We do not use data for purposes contrary to the poster's intent

4. **Lawful activity:**
   - Job aggregation is a legitimate business activity
   - Similar services operate legally (Indeed, Glassdoor, LinkedIn itself)

### Part B: Necessity Test

**Is the processing necessary for this purpose?**

YES. The processing is necessary because:

1. **No less intrusive alternative:**
   - To display job opportunities, we must process the poster's identifying information
   - Anonymous aggregation would defeat the purpose (candidates need to know who to contact)

2. **Minimal data collection:**
   - We only collect data that is publicly visible
   - We do not collect private profile information
   - We do not access data requiring authentication beyond public view

3. **Limited retention:**
   - Freelance opportunities: 14 days maximum
   - Structured job postings: 30 days maximum
   - Automatic deletion after expiry

4. **Purpose limitation:**
   - Data is used solely for job opportunity display
   - No secondary uses (advertising profiling, data selling, etc.)

### Part C: Balancing Test

**Do the individual's interests override our legitimate interest?**

NO. The balance favors processing because:

#### Factors in favor of processing:

1. **Reasonable expectations:**
   - When posting publicly on social media, individuals reasonably expect the content to be seen by third parties
   - Job postings are specifically intended to reach a wide audience
   - The poster's goal (finding candidates) aligns with our purpose (connecting candidates with opportunities)

2. **Nature of data:**
   - Professional/business context, not private life
   - Information voluntarily made public by the data subject
   - No special category data (health, religion, etc.)

3. **Impact on data subject:**
   - Positive impact: increased visibility for their job posting
   - No financial harm
   - No reputational harm (we display their own public content)

4. **Safeguards implemented:**
   - Easy opt-out mechanism (removal within 72 hours)
   - Short retention periods
   - Attribution and links to original source
   - No modification of original content
   - Privacy policy disclosure

#### Factors considered against processing:

1. **Lack of direct consent:**
   - Mitigated by: public nature of data, alignment with poster's intent, easy removal

2. **Data subject may change mind:**
   - Mitigated by: 72-hour removal process, short retention, automatic expiry

3. **Aggregation concern:**
   - Mitigated by: we aggregate job posts only, not personal profiles

---

## 3. Safeguards and Mitigations

### 3.1 Technical safeguards:
- Automatic deletion after 14 days (opportunities) / 30 days (jobs)
- No indexing of THIN quality content (noindex meta tag)
- Secure data storage

### 3.2 Organizational safeguards:
- Documented removal process
- 72-hour response time for removal requests
- Staff training on data protection
- Regular review of this assessment

### 3.3 Transparency measures:
- Privacy policy disclosure of data sources
- "Request Removal" link on every opportunity page
- Dedicated email for removal requests (removal@freelanly.com)
- Link to original source on every listing

### 3.4 Data subject rights:
- **Right to object:** Via removal request (removal@freelanly.com)
- **Right to erasure:** Honored within 72 hours
- **Right to information:** Privacy policy, this LIA document

---

## 4. Conclusion

Based on this assessment, we conclude that:

1. Processing serves a **legitimate purpose** that benefits both job posters and job seekers
2. Processing is **necessary** and proportionate, with no less intrusive alternative
3. The **balance of interests** favors processing, given:
   - Public nature of the data
   - Alignment with data subject's original intent
   - Implemented safeguards
   - Easy opt-out mechanism

**Decision:** Processing may proceed under Article 6(1)(f) GDPR (legitimate interests).
